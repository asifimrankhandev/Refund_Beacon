Refund Beacon

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

In short: Refund Beacon keeps everything you enter on your own device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. We never see your cases, receipts or reminders. Data leaves your device only when you choose to share it.

Who we are

Refund Beacon is published by idexa (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy, contact support@idexa.app.

What the app stores

Refund Beacon helps you organize a return whose refund hasn’t arrived. Everything you enter is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • Case details: retailer, item, amounts, dates, return method, carrier and tracking number.
  • Order numbers and private notes, if you add them.
  • Timeline events and refunds you record.
  • Evidence files you add: photos, PDFs and text files, with their names and notes.
  • Reminders you set, and your app preferences.

We do not have a copy of any of this. We cannot access, recover or delete it for you, because it never reaches us.

What we do not collect

Refund Beacon does not collect or transmit any personal data. There is no account or sign-in, and the app does not collect your name, email address, contacts or location. It does not connect to your email, bank, card or retailer accounts, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking. The app makes no network requests of its own. Links to official consumer resources open in your browser.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to share it, using your device’s share sheet or clipboard:

  • a case summary or CSV export (you choose which fields and files are included);
  • a full backup file;
  • a follow-up message you copy or share. The app never sends messages for you.

Whoever receives a shared file or message may store or forward it under their own policies. Backup files are not encrypted and contain your receipts and order details, so store them somewhere private.

Permissions

Permission Used for If you decline
Camera Only when you tap “Take photo” to add evidence. Choose an existing photo or file instead.
Storage (Android 9 and older) Requested with the camera so the system camera can save the photo. Choose an existing photo or file instead.
Notifications Only for reminders you set. Reminders still appear in the Reminders tab.

Photo access is not requested: the system photo picker shares only the photo you pick.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it:

  • Delete a single case from the case screen (Manage case › Delete case).
  • Delete everything from Settings › Delete all data on this device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Temporary copies made when you pick a file are removed automatically. Files you have already shared with others are outside the app and are not affected.

Children

Refund Beacon is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

What Refund Beacon is not

Refund Beacon organizes information you enter. It does not issue refunds, contact retailers, file disputes or give legal or financial advice, and it can’t guarantee any outcome.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@idexa.app. Please don’t email receipts or card details.
